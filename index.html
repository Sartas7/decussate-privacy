Decussate Neuroanatomy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Decussate does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Decussate Neuroanatomy ("Decussate", "the app") is an educational Android app for studying neuroanatomy, published by Ardist Inc. ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It requests no device permissions and works entirely offline. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Information stored on your device

To track your study progress, the app saves on your device which lessons you have completed, your quiz results, and which questions you missed. This information:

  • is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • can be erased at any time from About → Reset my progress in the app, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it;
  • may be included in your device's own backup (for example, Google backup) if you have turned that on. That backup is handled by your device's backup provider, not by us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Children

The app is intended for university and graduate students and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Educational use only

Decussate is a study aid. It is not medical advice and is not intended for diagnosing or treating any condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, we will update this policy before that version is released.
